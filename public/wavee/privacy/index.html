Wavee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Wavee is a messaging platform run by Vela Advisory ("we", "us"). Businesses use Wavee to send and receive WhatsApp messages with their customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta. This policy explains what data we handle, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Vela Advisory, [registered address], is responsible for the Wavee platform.
Contact: [contact email]

When a business uses Wavee to talk to its customers, that business decides how its customers' data is used. We process that data on the business's behalf and only to provide the service.

What data we handle

  • WhatsApp messages sent to and from a business's WhatsApp number through Wavee: text, voice notes, images and documents, with their date and time.
  • Contact details shared by WhatsApp: the phone number and the WhatsApp profile name.
  • Delivery information from WhatsApp: whether a message was sent, delivered or read.
  • Business account details for businesses that connect to Wavee: their WhatsApp Business account, phone number, message templates, and the name and email of the person who connects it.

We do not collect payment card details. Businesses pay Meta directly for WhatsApp messaging.

Why we use it

  • To deliver messages between a business and its customers.
  • To show a business its conversations, and to help it keep track of follow-ups and commitments in them.
  • To create and manage message templates for the business.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by checking incoming messages for harmful content.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages.
  • Service providers that host our servers or process text for us (for example, AI services that transcribe voice notes or summarise conversations), under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing that service.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact details for as long as the business uses Wavee, or until the business or the person asks us to delete them. When a business disconnects from Wavee, we delete its data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

How we protect it

Data is stored on secured servers and sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited to the people who run the service.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to stop processing it. Email [contact email]. If you are a customer of a business that uses Wavee, you can also contact that business directly. You can stop receiving messages from a business at any time by blocking its number in WhatsApp.

Data deletion

To have your data deleted, email [contact email] with the phone number or account concerned. We confirm the deletion within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
